# E18 - the ordinal head versus the simplex head, on the decision

> **Disclosure.** A further development use of the reserved panel. Component decisions taken here need a sealed surface to become confirmatory.

## Why fold 0 cannot answer this

The manuscript attributes +0.0092 pooled Spearman to the ordinal head, established at a development resolution of about 0.005. The decision differences that separate methods are an order of magnitude smaller. On fold 0's 546 informative decision groups the simplex-headed member beats the ordinal-headed one by +0.00075, CI [-0.00156, +0.00284], p = 0.504 — no power at all. The panel offers 30,475 groups.
